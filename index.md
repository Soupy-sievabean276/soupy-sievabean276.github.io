## 📊 Understanding your results

The application gives you a score based on two main factors: linguistic fit and market reception.

*   Linguistic Fit: This score shows how well the name performs within your chosen cultural origin. It checks for pronunciation, tone, and cultural meaning.
*   Market Reception: This score predicts how your target market sees the name. It tests for clarity, ease of memory, and brand appeal.

If the score is low, the tool provides specific reasons for the result. It identifies if the name might be hard for your target market to spell or if it carries an unintended meaning in another language. You can then try a different name or modify your current choice and run the test again.

## 💡 Best practices for naming

*   Keep the name simple. Names that are easy to spell stay in the memory of customers longer.
*   Check the domain availability. Once you find a name with a good score, check if the website address is open.
*   Avoid symbols. Names with numbers or special characters often cause confusion.
*   Focus on the brand story. A good name links your background to the goal of the business.
